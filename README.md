# Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes telecom customer data to understand customer churn patterns and identify factors associated with customer attrition.

## Business Problem
The telecom company is experiencing customer churn and wants to understand which customer groups are more likely to leave its services.

## Project Objectives
- Calculate the overall customer churn rate.
- Analyze churn by contract type.
- Understand churn patterns across customer tenure.
- Analyze payment methods and internet services.
- Provide data-driven customer retention recommendations.

## Dataset Information
- Total Records: 7,043
- Domain: Telecommunications
- Analysis Focus: Customer churn and customer behavior.

## Tools & Technologies
- Excel
- MySQL
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## Key Business Findings
- Overall customer churn rate: 26.54%.
- Month-to-month contract churn rate: 42.71%.
- Two-year contract churn rate: 2.83%.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus retention efforts on month-to-month contract customers.
- Monitor customer churn during the first year.
- Investigate customer behavior and service factors associated with churn.

## Project Workflow
1. Data cleaning and preparation.
2. Exploratory data analysis.
3. SQL-based business analysis.
4. KPI calculations.
5. Power BI dashboard development.
6. Business insights and recommendations.
